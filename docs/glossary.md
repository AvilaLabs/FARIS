# Glossary

Terms FARIS uses in the app, the command line and the handbook. Each is defined once, in at most 40 words, with a
link to the page that explains it. `scripts/check_glossary.py` checks the length, the links, that no term repeats,
and that every verdict word and result label FARIS shows is here.

- **PASS.** A check ran within its stated scope and met its criterion. It says nothing outside that scope. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **FAIL.** A check ran within its stated scope and did not meet its criterion, or it produced an error. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **INCONCLUSIVE.** A check ran but its evidence is too weak to decide, for example a sampling error that is too large. It is not a pass. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **NOT_EVALUATED.** FARIS makes no claim either way, because the check was not run or the inputs to it are missing. Every one says why and what to do next. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **calculated.** A value computed by the FARIS engine or by a recorded solver run from stated inputs. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **checked.** A value for which a numerical control or check passed within its declared scope. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **authored.** An assumption written for this scenario by a person. It can be changed and is not a measurement. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **literature.** A value taken from cited literature. Citing a source does not make the value suitable for your design. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **conditional.** A value that holds only under stated conditions, such as the cold-data surrogate or the authored operating assumptions. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **partial.** A value for which a precision or coverage goal is not met. Use it with care. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **failed.** The label shown when a check failed or an error occurred. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **not evaluated.** The label form of NOT_EVALUATED, shown on a value FARIS makes no claim about. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **Research screening.** FARIS results are for early comparison of designs. They are not a licensing, safety or design basis. [Scope and limits](guide/scope.md)
- **Scenario.** One definition of geometry, materials, solver inputs and component identities, from which every calculation starts. [Design: the plant in 3D](guide/design.md)
- **Study.** A saved piece of work: its scenario, recorded transport, assumptions, ensembles and the view you left open, stored in a `.faris` file. [Study files](guide/study-files.md)
- **Allocation.** How the blanket and shield thickness is split between the two roles for one design. The sweep compares seven splits. [Compare and the allocation sweep](guide/compare.md)
- **Port.** The opening through the blanket and shield, here an open outboard duct, that lets neutrons stream toward the magnets. [Scope and limits](guide/scope.md)
- **Control (port-free).** The same design without the port, run to show what the port changes. [Compare and the allocation sweep](guide/compare.md)
- **Transport.** Following neutrons through the geometry by Monte Carlo to tally reaction rates, heating and flux. [Simulate: transport results](guide/simulate.md)
- **History (operating history).** The plant over its life, year by year, built from the transport rates and the authored operating assumptions. [Operate: the 30-year history](guide/operate.md)
- **Cold-data surrogate.** Transport with nuclear data at one nominal temperature and authored stand-in materials, not a design's real materials. [Scope and limits](guide/scope.md)
- **Standard error (SE).** The sampling error of one Monte Carlo run. A small value means the quantity was well sampled, not that the model is right. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **2σ flag.** The screening flag that compares a difference of two runs with twice the combined standard error. It is not a significance test. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **Ensemble.** Many repeats of the operating history with the transport sampling uncertainty drawn in each, giving a median and a 5 to 95 per cent range. [Uncertainty ensembles](guide/uncertainty.md)
- **Tritium breeding ratio (TBR).** Tritium made per fusion neutron. The demo reports a gross whole-model tritium rate, not a breeder-only ratio. [Scope and limits](guide/scope.md)
- **Fast fluence.** The time-integrated flux of fast neutrons. The magnet service limit is checked against it as a regional average. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **Service limit.** A screening value for how much damage a part is assumed to take before replacement. It is not a material allowable. [Reading the numbers](guide/results.md)
- **Activation.** Making material radioactive by neutron capture. FARIS uses it to time maintenance through decay heat. [Computed maintenance durations](guide/maintenance.md)
- **Decay heat.** The heat a part gives off from its radioactivity after shutdown. It sets how long a cooldown takes. [Computed maintenance durations](guide/maintenance.md)
- **Outage.** The time the plant is down for a replacement: the cooldown plus the work time. [Computed maintenance durations](guide/maintenance.md)
- **Receipt.** A hash-bound record from Avila Core that ties a result to its exact inputs. [Evidence and Avila Core receipts](guide/evidence.md)
- **Evidence.** The receipts and checks behind a result. Compilation, run readiness and the scientific verdict are kept as three separate lines. [Evidence and Avila Core receipts](guide/evidence.md)
- **Avila Core.** The Avila Labs tool that compiles a case and records receipts. FARIS shows a small attribution whenever Core compiled something. [Evidence and Avila Core receipts](guide/evidence.md)
- **ACTINV.** The Avila Labs activation and decay-heat solver that FARIS asks for the maintenance calculation. [Computed maintenance durations](guide/maintenance.md)
- **OpenMC.** The external Monte Carlo code that performs the transport runs. FARIS does not ship it. [External tools and their licences](guide/external-tools.md)
- **Nuclear data.** The cross-section library a transport run reads. It is large, so FARIS fetches it by hash. [Nuclear data](guide/nuclear-data.md)
- **Design file.** The file that brings your own CAD geometry into FARIS. [The design file](guide/design-file.md)
- **Bundle (transport bundle).** The folder of inputs and results for a transport run that you supply or FARIS records. [Run your own transport](guide/transport.md)
- **Hash (SHA-256).** A fingerprint of a file's bytes. It shows the file is the one that was recorded, not that its contents are right. [Download and verify](guide/install.md)
