## Evidence boundaries

- `controls/check_transport_arithmetic.py` checks arithmetic and volume conversions independently with Decimal precision. It does not check Monte Carlo correctness or physical model validity.
- `controls/check_history.py` checks history equations and balance independently. `references/portable-history-comparison-verification.json` also records independent Decimal terminal mass-balance and signed-ledger checks for all 12 outage-duration cases. These checks do not qualify the transport inputs, outage assumptions, service limits, or scenario physics.
- `controls/pure_absorber_sphere.py` and `controls/test_pure_absorber_sphere.py` exercise an independent OpenMC numerical-control problem. They do not establish accuracy for this toroidal design.
- `controls/check_port_geometry.py` is a geometry-volume cross-check. Adaptive quadrature, midpoint convergence, and point classification still require comparison at the exact port scenario/run identity; agreement cannot validate materials or nuclear data.
- Core fixture assessments in `runs/core-integration-controls-001.json` test Core semantics and FARIS declaration binding only. A fixture `PASS` is not a physical FARIS `PASS`.
- The fresh solver cancellation control in `references/native-solver-cancellation-control.json` records an intentionally incomplete run; it has no normalized response and contributes no transport result.
- Any display of history inventory, service events, or net power must identify the assumptions and underlying transport run. Missing whole-model heating leaves recovered heat and electricity unavailable.

## Final release gate

The scoped functional/numerical demo gate is complete: the canonical package is indexed and revalidated, recorded cases and Core evidence replay, and native checks cover study editing, fields, timeline events, sensitivity, cancellation, recalculation invalidation/recovery, and minimum-scale keyboard access. This conclusion applies only to the tested Linux workstation and documented cold-model boundary; it does not imply reactor physics validation or cross-host/platform behavior. Before making physical or engineering claims, obtain response-specific external validation, resolve the missed local precision and mesh-refinement criteria, establish authenticated data provenance and applicable material/service limits, propagate response uncertainty/covariance through histories and comparisons, and close neutron-source plus nuclear-Q energy accounting. Until then, retain `NOT_EVALUATED` for reactor physics and do not present deterministic comparisons or authored outage/service probes as a design ranking, lifetime, availability, or safety result.
