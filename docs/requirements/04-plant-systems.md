# Plant systems

What FARIS must model around the radiation field: magnets and cryogenics (MAG), thermal-hydraulics and heat exhaust (THM), the tritium fuel cycle (FUEL), power balance and the 0D plasma (PWR), economics (ECO) and safety screening (SAFE). Reference hardware and models (RL, RW, RM-S, RM-M, RM-L) are defined in the [index](README.md#reference-hardware-and-models). Speed targets for coupled recalculation live in PERF-020 and are not restated here. Accuracy against published benchmarks is decided in the validation file and uncertainty propagation in the uncertainty file; this file describes the capability and names the reference case. Operating history, maintenance and availability are in the operation-and-life file; shutdown dose is in the radiation-transport file.

Today FARIS has a deterministic 30-year ledger (tritium inventory with two compartments, energy ledger with one auxiliary load, authored fluence limits with replacement outages). It has no magnet, cryogenic, thermal-hydraulic, cost or safety model. Rows below say exactly what exists. Every number FARIS shows in these areas carries one kind label (calculated, authored, literature, conditional, not-evaluated), and every FARIS output here is research screening, never a licensing or safety claim.

## Traps

- Required TBR quoted as one number ("1.05–1.15") hides burn fraction, fuelling efficiency, processing time, availability and reserve. Show the surface and carry the systematic term.
- Q_eng and net efficiency differ between PROCESS, ARIES and FUSE. A closed power balance only proves that terms add up; a missing load closes just as well.
- A nuclear heating total under the cryo budget can hide a local winding-pack hotspot, and a 1-sigma tally is not a margin. Carnot-only cryo power understates the penalty by 3 to 4 times.
- Fluence without energy cut-off, temperature or annealing history is not comparable. Limits such as 1e7 Gy, 150 K, 660 MPa or 10 MW/m² belong to a named design, not to the material.
- A divertor steady limit is a component rating. Unmitigated average flux compared with it, or a loop with liquid-metal MHD left out, looks good and means nothing.
- A cost of electricity from synthetic prices is a model exercise; reproducing ARIES-ACT proves the code, not the plant.
- A tool that prints "meets NRC limits" creates false confidence. Waste class depends on regime, geometry and impurity specifications, and a proposed rule is not a rule.
- A passing conservation check (mass, energy, cost closure) shows arithmetic, not physics.
