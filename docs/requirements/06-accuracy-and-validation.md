# Accuracy and validation

How FARIS shows that its numbers are right, and how wrong they may be. **Verification** asks whether the code solves its equations correctly (manufactured solutions, analytic benchmarks, closure tests). **Validation** asks whether those equations describe nature (experiments). Both are needed, and neither is claimed from the other. Every calculated quantity maps to a benchmark with a stated bias and uncertainty, and a blank row blocks release. Plant-level reproduction cases (PWR, FUEL, ECO) live in [04-plant-systems](04-plant-systems.md); this file only says how they are scored. Uncertainty methods live in [07-uncertainty](07-uncertainty.md). Reference hardware and models are in the [index](README.md#reference-hardware-and-models).

Two rules run through the file. First, FARIS reports the distribution of C/E (calculated over experiment) and the worst case, never only the mean. Second, FARIS outputs are research screening. No result here is a licensing or safety claim, and agreement with an experiment is evidence for that response only.

## Traps

- **Small statistical error is not accuracy.** It measures sampling only. Geometry holes, undersampled regions and biased weight windows give small errors and wrong values (VAL-028, VAL-012).
- **Code-to-code agreement is not validation.** Shared nuclear data and shared geometry mistakes agree perfectly and are both wrong (VAL-041).
- **Mean C/E hides the worst detector.** One deep detector at 1.4 can sit beside nine at 1.0. Show the distribution, the worst case and the trend against depth (VAL-030, VAL-032).
- **Benchmark count is not coverage.** More than 2,000 benchmarks says nothing about whether one is similar to the user's design. Use similarity, and list what is not covered (VAL-072, VAL-075).
- **Fission accuracy numbers do not transfer.** Reactivity in pcm and boron in ppm come from decades of plant data. Fusion has none, so the only honest bars are experiment, code-to-code and published reproduction (VAL-081, VAL-083).
- **Literature C/E is not our C/E.** Published agreement used a specific code, library and model. Run and record your own, and mark them stale when anything changes (VAL-036, VAL-037).
- **The fast tier is only as good as its test box.** A 1 s recalculation outside the sampled slider range, or after an edit that needs new transport, has no stored error and must say so (VAL-063, VAL-066).
- **A loose acceptance band chosen after the fact is not a verdict.** The rule is fixed before the result, and uncertainty drives the pass, not an observed percentage (VAL-005, VAL-027).
