# Radiation transport and neutron source

How FARIS runs neutron and photon transport, what it computes from it, how it knows a tally
has converged, and how the fusion neutron source is defined and normalised. NUC covers
transport; SRC covers the source and wall loading. Hardware and reference models RM-S, RM-M
and RM-L are in [the index](README.md#reference-hardware-and-models); run-time and scaling
targets are in [08-performance.md](08-performance.md) (PERF-023 to PERF-035) and are not
repeated. Accuracy against benchmarks (SINBAD, FNG, FNS, JET) belongs in
06-accuracy-and-validation.md, and uncertainty propagation (nuclear data, geometry) in
07-uncertainty.md; this file states the capabilities those files rely on. Geometry integrity
is in [01-geometry-and-model.md](01-geometry-and-model.md). Activation and decay heat are in
[03-activation-and-materials.md](03-activation-and-materials.md). A small statistical error is
a statement about sampling only; it says nothing about geometry, data or variance-reduction bias.

## Traps

- A small relative error is not accuracy. Geometry holes, undersampled regions and biased weight windows all give small errors and wrong values. Pair it with the variance of the variance, FOM and an analogue cross-check.
- Photon-filtered heating looks like a heating number but is about 1e-4 of the physical value in OpenMC. Only the sum over neutron, photon, electron and positron is heating.
- Per-source-neutron values and per-second values both look like "flux". Multiplying twice by the source strength is easy and silent.
- FOM gain from variance reduction can be illusory when the windows were tuned on the same histories as the tally.
- Passing the statistical checks at 1 M histories does not mean the deep magnet tally converged; most histories never reach it.
- Mixed neutron and photon libraries silently change gamma heating and shutdown dose. The result still looks smooth.
- A tally with no scores shows zero or zero error. That is an absence of evidence, not a zero dose.
- A source with a uniform ring and a single energy is tidy and biases the first-wall flux. State it as a simplification every time.
