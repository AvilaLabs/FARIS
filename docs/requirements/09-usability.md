# Usability

How easy FARIS is to learn, drive, trust and recover in. Targets are measured with
named participants and recorded scripts, not opinion. Timing and frame budgets live in
[PERFORMANCE](08-performance.md) and are referred to by ID. Crash recovery and file
integrity live in the reliability file (18-reliability, prefix REL); this file only
states what the user sees. Reference hardware is in [the index](README.md#reference-hardware-and-models).
"New domain user" means a fusion or nuclear engineer or graduate student who has not
used FARIS and was not on the team. "Core tasks" are defined in UX-010.

## Traps

- SUS is a satisfaction score, not a diagnosis. A mean from five people, or from the team, is noise. Report the CI and the sample, and benchmark only with outsiders.
- SUS rewards familiarity: ParaView users rate ParaView well. The 68 average comes from a web-heavy database, not engineering software.
- Task success with hints counts as failure. Count assisted tasks separately or success rates inflate.
- Onboarding completion rises when the tour gets shorter. Measure what people remember and whether their first own study works.
- "Instant" feedback on a slider says nothing about edits that need a transport run. Always show the cost class before the edit.
- Undo coverage measured by menu items misses drags, drops and background changes. Test from the registry with random sequences and compare study hashes.
- A shortcut count looks like keyboard support. Mouse-free completion of the whole task sequence is the test.
- Counting "no bare N/A" by string search misses empty cells and dashes. Scan exports and screenshots too.
